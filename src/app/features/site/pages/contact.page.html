Support
Use the form here, signed in or not. If you are signed in, the dashboard's feedback page does the same thing and attaches your page and app version automatically, which is what makes a bug reproducible. Both go to the same inbox.
Teams and education
Team plans can be invoiced annually instead of paid by card; pick “Invoicing” and tell us the seats and the billing entity. Students: set your role to Student in your personal info, then get in touch with your institution and we will apply the discount.
Security and privacy
Your drawings stay in your account and on your device. A summary of a drawing reaches an AI provider only when you configure one yourself in the assistant's settings; the built-in parser and a local Ollama server send nothing anywhere. Details in the Privacy Policy. To report a vulnerability, use the form and start with the word “Security”.
