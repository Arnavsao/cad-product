Privacy Policy

Status: draft

1. What we collect

  • Account data: handled by Supabase (email, auth identifiers, session tokens).
  • Drawing data: the DXF/drawing content you save, stored in our database and object storage.
  • Usage data: basic operational logs (requests, errors) needed to run the service.

2. AI features

If you choose the local (Ollama) model, your drawing data never leaves your machine/network. If you choose OpenRouter, a summary of your drawing (layers, entity counts/types, current selection — not the raw file) and your prompts are sent to OpenRouter to generate a response. This only happens after you've selected that model and acknowledged the in-app notice.

3. Cookies

Supabase stores the session token your browser needs for sign-in to work. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

We don't sell your data. It's shared only with the infrastructure providers needed to run the service (hosting, database, object storage, and — only if you opt in — your chosen AI provider).
