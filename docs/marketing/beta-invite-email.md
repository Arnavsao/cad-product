# CADO open beta — email copy

Two emails: a cold invitation to people who have not seen CADO, and a shorter
note to existing sign-ups. Plain text, no images, one link each. Fill the
bracketed fields.

---

## 1. Invitation — for drafters, architects, engineers, fabricators

**Subject options** (pick one)

- CADO is in open beta. Free, in the browser, and we want your feedback
- A 2D drafting tool that opens in a tab. Would you try it and tell us what breaks?
- Free 2D CAD in the browser, open beta

**Body**

Hi [first name],

I am [your name], one of the people building CADO. It is a 2D drafting editor
that runs entirely in the browser: open a DXF exactly as AutoCAD drew it, draft
with the same commands and aliases, lay it out on paper, plot to PDF, and write
the DXF back out. The whole editor works in 14 languages, command prompts
included.

We opened the beta to everyone this week. It is free while it is in beta. There
is nothing to install, no credits, and no card.

What I would ask of you is thirty minutes with one of your own drawings:

1. Open cado.website and sign in.
2. Upload a DXF you know well and check what looks different from your desktop CAD.
3. Make a few edits the way you normally would, then plot a PDF.
4. Tell us what broke, what was missing, and what felt slow: cado.website/feedback

The feedback page attaches your app version automatically, which is what makes a
bug reproducible. Reports go straight to the team, and if yours changes the
product you will hear back from us.

Two things you should know before you start. DWG files can be stored and
downloaded but not yet opened, so save as DXF from your desktop CAD first. And
the AI drafting assistant uses a model key you provide, so it is optional.

Thank you for the time. It is exactly the kind of feedback we cannot get any
other way.

[your name]
[role], CADO
cado.website

## Notes

- One link per email. Everything points at cado.website or cado.website/feedback.
- Do not attach images or a deck. A plain email from a person gets read.
- Send from a personal address at the company, not a no-reply.
- Keep "free while in beta". Do not promise what pricing will be after.
