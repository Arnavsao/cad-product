## Variant A — plain announcement (recommended)

CADO is in open beta, and it is free for everyone while it is.

It is a full 2D drafting editor that runs in a browser tab. Nothing to install,
no licence, no credits, no card. Open a DXF exactly as AutoCAD drew it, draft
with the same commands and aliases you already type, lay it out on paper, plot
to PDF, and write the DXF back out. The whole editor, command prompts included,
works in the same 14 languages AutoCAD ships in.

We are opening it up now because we want it in front of people who draft for a
living. What is missing, what feels wrong, what you reached for and could not
find. Every report goes to the people building it, and the ones that change the
product get a reply.

Try it: cado.website
Tell us what broke: cado.website/feedback

## Follow-up comment (post it yourself under the announcement)

What is in the beta today: 70+ drafting commands, blocks with attributes,
dimensions and dimension styles, hatches, tables, paper layouts with viewports,
26 paper sizes, PDF and SVG plot, DXF import and export from R12 to 2018, an
AI drafting assistant you connect to your own model key.

What is not yet: opening DWG directly (store and download only, save as DXF from
your desktop CAD for now), real-time multi-user editing. Both are on the list.
